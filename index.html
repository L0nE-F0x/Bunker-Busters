01

Stealth over shootouts

Crouch past tin-can tripwires, wait out a guard drone's battery brown-outs, kill the floodlights or go in under the cover of night. Every layer of a bunker has more than one way through.

02

Pick every lock by feel

A real pin-tumbler minigame: find the binding pin, set it at the shear line, don't overset or your pick snaps. Engineers can short keypads instead, and anyone can guess a founder's default PIN.

03

A living wasteland

A full day/night cycle, dust storms rolling over the dunes, godrays through broken power lines and neon flickering out in the dark. Everything is generated in code: no textures, no asset packs.

Tier 1 bunker

The Garage

Owner: Tanner Pivotson, founder & Chief Visionary of Bunkr.ly ("Uber for Bunkers")

He raised $40M on a napkin and built exactly one bunker: his own. It's guarded by SeedBot, a refurbished security drone with 12% battery health, a padlock with a five-star rating, and a laser grid wired to a fuse box nobody labelled.

"This property is pre-revenue but post-apocalypse. Leave."
  • Razor-wire perimeter: pick the gate or find the gap the raccoons use
  • SeedBot patrols with a vision cone, gets suspicious, and tases on sight
  • Laser tripwires: jump the low beam, crouch under the high one
  • The Runway Room: loot crates, a safe and a 30-under-30 shrine
