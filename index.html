BUNKERBUSTERS

They built bunkers. You brought lockpicks.

The founders saw the end coming and sealed themselves in with the region's water. You live outside, at a camp by a dead gas station. Scout their doors, get past their half-broken security, and carry what you can back to the fire.

How a job goes

Scout. Get in. Get out.

  1. 01

    Scout

    Glass the place from a ridge. Binoculars tag every camera, tripwire, laser and contractor on the fence. Notes, logs and gossip give away the codes the owners thought were clever.

  2. 02

    Get in

    Every door has more than one answer. Pick the lock by feel, splice the terminal, short the keypad, talk your way past the intercom, or set a charge and accept that everyone heard it.

  3. 03

    Get out

    The alarm calls Kade Recovery. Their crews take cover, flank and radio for help while you carry the loot, and the water, back across the flats to camp.

Security

Security on a budget

SeedBot patrols the Garage on 12% battery health and a taser. Cameras sweep, lasers hang off unlabelled fuse boxes, and the owner shouts at you over the speakers while you work.

Hacking

SPLICE

A terminal minigame at every Kade outpost: switch off sentries, land drones, call in a resupply, open a vault.

Enemies

Kade Recovery

Contractors in hard hats and respirators. They take cover, flank, rush you while you reload, and sometimes put their hands up. Marksmen on the towers give themselves away with a scope glint. Breachers walk straight through your cover.

Wildlife

The desert bites

Wolf packs hunt at night and flinch from your torch. Rattlesnakes, scorpions, coyotes, and vultures over whatever you leave behind. Food and water run down as you walk.

Story

Every line is spoken

Two acts so far, and 865 voiced lines: Mara's briefings at the fire, Dez's rumours on the radio, Dry Creek's diner gossip, and bunker owners who narrate your break-in as it happens.

World

A desert that keeps time

A full day and night, dust storms you can see coming, and a survey map that fills in as you explore. Fast travel between camps costs the food and water the walk would have.

Your kit

Four characters, five guns

An Infiltrator, an Engineer, a Brute or a Fixer, each with their own skills. A crowbar, the Six-Shooter, the Pump Twelve, a Lever .30-30 and the suppressed Hush .22, with a scope, a choke and a speedloader made at the fire. Molotovs, binoculars, plates and bandages.
