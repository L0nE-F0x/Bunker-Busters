Descriptions

About the game

Short

Bunker Busters is a free first-person heist game set in a desert valley where the founders sealed themselves into doomsday bunkers with the region's water. You live at a camp by a dead gas station. Scout each bunker, get past its half-broken security and the contractors who guard it, and carry the water back to camp.

Long

The founders saw the end coming. On Pivot Day they sealed themselves into their bunkers, and the region's water went in with them. Three and a half years later, the people left outside live at a camp by Last Chance Gas with one radio and not much to drink.

You're the one the camp sends. Pick one of six people (an infiltrator, an engineer, a brute, a fixer, a scout, or a defector who once had a seat in a bunker) and take the bunkers one at a time: a startup founder's padlocked garage, a rocket hangar, a lighthouse full of cameras, a tower whose turrets apologise before they fire, a glass dome that patches every trick you use against it, a fulfilment centre built like a cathedral, a star fort in a moat of coolant, and last, the bunker of the woman who built all the others.

Every door has more than one way through. Pick the lock, splice the terminal, short the keypad, talk your way past the intercom, or blow it open and deal with Kade Recovery when they arrive. Between jobs there's a desert with a full day and night, dust storms, wolf packs, a town and a dozen places to scavenge. All of the dialogue is voiced. It runs on Linux and Windows, in the browser and on phones, and it's free.

Features

What's in it

  • Eight bunkers over eight acts, each with its own owner, its own security and more than one way in.
  • Six characters to play, each with their own skills, starting kit and reason to go.
  • Lockpicking by feel, SPLICE terminal hacking, keypad codes hidden in notes, intercoms you can talk your way past, and breach charges.
  • Kade Recovery crews who take cover, flank, call for help and sometimes surrender, with marksmen, breachers and grenadiers.
  • A desert that keeps time: a full day and night, dust storms, wolf packs, rattlesnakes, scorpions, and food and water that run down as you walk.
  • Twelve places off the highway, from Dry Creek's diner to a crashed private jet, on a survey map that fills in as you explore.
  • More than 1,300 voiced lines: briefings at the fire, rumours on the radio, and bunker owners who talk to you while you break in.
  • Plays anywhere: keyboard and mouse, gamepad with aim assist, and touch controls on phones. Free, with no ads and no account.
